Cancellation, Terms & Privacy

Learn how cancellations, refunds, booking confirmation and guest privacy are handled for every Sunset Dunes experience.

Flexible Cancellation Policy

Changes or cancellations made at least 48 hours before the scheduled tour start time are eligible for a full refund. Cancellation requests received within 48 hours will be reviewed and may be subject to a partial charge, depending on supplier availability and operational commitments.

  • Full refund for cancellations more than 48 hours before departure.
  • Partial refund or rescheduling support for 24-48 hours notice.
  • No refund for cancellations within 24 hours, unless caused by weather or force majeure.
  • Tour confirmations are issued after payment processes and are subject to availability.

Terms & Conditions

By booking with Sunset Dunes Tours, you agree to our safety procedures, operational terms, and customer service standards. We reserve the right to modify tour itineraries for weather, safety, or regulatory reasons.

  • All guests must carry valid identification and abide by local regulations.
  • Private vehicle upgrades incur an additional flat fee and must be confirmed during booking.
  • Tour pricing includes the selected package tier, vehicle option and applicable taxes unless otherwise noted.
  • We are not liable for lost personal belongings during excursions.

Privacy & Data Use

Your contact and booking details are used only to confirm reservations, send travel information, and manage supplier arrangements. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

  • Booking details are stored securely in our systems.
  • Uploaded photos are used only to support your tour request and are not publicly shared.
  • We may use your contact details to send important trip updates or customer service messages.
